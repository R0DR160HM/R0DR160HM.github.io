This site is better in motion

It is set in a 3D night sea: the camera flies over the water as you scroll, a lighthouse sweeps its beam, and the constellations light up as you pass.

Your device is asking websites to reduce motion, so all of that would hold still. If you set that on purpose, go right in: everything works, it just stays still.

Turn animations on

This page notices the change and takes you in; if it doesn’t, reload it.

Windows your system

Open Settings › Accessibility › Visual effects and turn on Animation effects.

On Windows 10: Settings › Ease of Access › Display, then turn on Show animations in Windows.

macOS your system

Open System Settings › Accessibility › Display and turn off Reduce motion.

iPhone and iPad your system

Open Settings › Accessibility › Motion and turn off Reduce Motion.

Android your system

Open Settings › Accessibility and turn off Remove animations. On recent Pixel phones it is under Color and motion; on Samsung phones, under Visibility enhancements.

Linux your system

GNOME: open Settings › Accessibility › Seeing and turn off Reduce Animation. On older versions, turn on Tweaks › General › Animations.

KDE Plasma: open System Settings › Workspace Behavior › General Behavior and move Animation speed away from Instant.

Firefox can also be told to reduce motion on its own. If this page stays after you change your system, open about:config and set ui.prefersReducedMotion to 0, or delete it.
